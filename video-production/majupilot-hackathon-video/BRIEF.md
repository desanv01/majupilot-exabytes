## Intent

Create a faceless, English-only hackathon prototype video for MajuPilot. The film should
make the product legible as a trustworthy decision workflow for Malaysian SMEs: capture a
small evidence set, preserve what is known and unknown, calculate a deterministic path,
compare scenarios, generate a reviewable Blueprint, and offer a consented handoff. The
approved duration window is 9:35–9:45; the working target is 9:40. The functional prototype
demonstration must follow the authentic production app and occupy a realistic 3:37 window
inside the longer cut.

The required competition sections are Problem / Objectives, Proposed Solution, Technical
Approach / Design, Functional Prototype Demonstration, Testing / Validation, and Industry
Value / Future Potential. The video is faceless and may use UI capture, diagrams, typographic
explanation, and evidence cards. No final narration copy or scene build is locked in this
phase.
