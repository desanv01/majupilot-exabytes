## 3. Classification and activation rules

- Classification is required and singular. Provider, commercial status, source date, prerequisites, and caveats are separate fields.
- `Exabytes product` means the official page presents it as an Exabytes-named offering. `partner/resold product` means a named third-party service is sold/offered through Exabytes. `supported path` means official Exabytes evidence supports a route but not a direct catalogue SKU. `third-party alternative` is explicitly non-Exabytes. `consultation-only` is a need with no verified product mapping. `unavailable/unverified` fails closed.
- An active entry needs a reachable official page reviewed within the configured freshness window (default 90 days), an approved summary, and a reviewer. Expiry disables new mappings without changing old Blueprint versions.
- Prices, discounts, uptime, testimonials, usage statistics, and plan inclusions are not copied into recommendations unless separately approved for a time-bounded use. Default pricing remains “verify current quote with Exabytes.”
- Official blog articles may support existence/context but do not activate a product when a current product page or portfolio entry is absent.
