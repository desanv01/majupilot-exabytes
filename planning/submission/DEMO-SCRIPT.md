# Timed demonstration script (target 8:45)

## 0:00–0:45 — Problem

Malaysian SMEs often know they should digitalise but cannot defend the sequence, prerequisites, cost, or likely operational value. A generic chat answer cannot preserve evidence or reproduce the arithmetic.

## 0:45–1:25 — Solution and trust boundary

Introduce SME Growth Twin as an explainable decision workflow. Point out that fictional demo data is clearly labelled, calculations are deterministic, and model failure has a five-role fallback.

## 1:25–2:10 — Load Case A and review the Business Twin

On `/`, select the recommended fictional Kopi Kita case. Show the persistent demo banner and the review page. Explain that only an assessment draft was loaded; no diagnosis, Blueprint, consent, or lead was pre-created.

## 2:10–3:15 — Diagnosis

Confirm the Business Twin. On `/results`, show digital maturity `37.5`, AI readiness `42.5`, evidence links, missing evidence, and the ordered pain points. Open one disclosure.

## 3:15–4:20 — Recommendations

On `/recommendations`, show capability-first sequencing, official-source links, and why governed AI is later. Explain the catalogue/version boundary and no promotional-price claim.

## 4:20–5:25 — Scenarios and ROI

On `/scenarios`, compare Lean, Balanced, and Accelerated. Select Balanced Growth. Show Case A first-year cost `9,200 / 18,400 / 27,600` and payback `7.2 / 30.4 / 140.5` months. Edit one assumption if recording time permits and state that values are conditional planning estimates.

## 5:25–6:35 — Advisor panel and Blueprint

Generate the Blueprint with model credentials absent. Show five distinct deterministic fallback roles, preserved disagreement/conditions, 16 sections, provenance, versions, and limitations. Explain the auditable ceiling: five roles, one retry per role, 900 output tokens per attempt, 12 seconds per role, at most ten attempts/9,000 output tokens per request.

## 6:35–7:25 — Consultation privacy

Open `/consultation`. Show unchecked consent and the exact data disclosure. Submit fictional contact data, then show the safe receipt without echoing contact details. State the honest process-local/no-email/no-CRM boundary.

## 7:25–8:05 — Engineering proof

Show `TEST-EVIDENCE.md`: A/B/C golden assertions, zero serious/critical axe findings on representative states, desktop/360 overflow and target checks, clean console/network, CSP/security headers, production audit, fallback journey duration, and prior Stage 05/06 browser gates.

## 8:05–8:45 — Impact, originality, limits, reset

Show the architecture diagram and explain the staged responsibilities of the original SME domain model. State limitations without qualification, reset known demo keys, and close with the SME/consultant value: an inspectable transformation plan and evidence-ready handoff.
