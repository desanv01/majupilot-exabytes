# Judge Q&A

## How are scores calculated?

Versioned TypeScript rules calculate weighted digital-maturity and AI-readiness dimensions from schema-validated Business Twin evidence. Missing answers lower confidence instead of becoming zero. The Results evidence drawers and golden tests expose the contributing records and exact values.

## Where does AI add value?

Optional AI produces five role-specific, schema-validated critiques. It cannot change facts, scores, product mappings, costs, ROI, schedules, or state. When credentials or a role call fail, the matching deterministic advisor completes the panel.

## What prevents hallucinated product recommendations?

The deterministic engine selects a capability first, enforces prerequisites and budget policy, and only then maps it to the reviewed Exabytes Catalogue 1.0.0 allow-list. Model output cannot introduce offerings.

## How accurate is ROI?

It is a transparent scenario estimate, not a forecast or guarantee. The engine exposes low/base/high assumptions, formulas, exclusions, cost, gross operational value, net value, and payback. Revenue and avoided-risk value remain “not estimated” until all required inputs exist.

## How is this different from ChatGPT?

The application preserves a typed Business Twin, deterministic scoring and sequencing, versioned catalogue facts, replayable scenarios, evidence references, advisor disagreements, a source-linked Blueprint, and explicit consent. The same inputs reproduce the same numbers.

## What data reaches an external model?

Only the bounded advisor review context can reach the configured server-side Gateway. Contact details are not part of that context. Credentials remain server-only. The demo works with no model credentials.

## Can Exabytes update the catalogue?

Yes. Catalogue facts and provider-specific recommendation/mapping policy live in a versioned Exabytes domain pack, separate from the reusable deterministic core. A new reviewed version requires tests and provenance updates.

## How does the lead reach sales?

In this prototype it does not. Explicit consent creates a process-local record and safe receipt only. Durable storage, notification, CRM delivery, authentication, retention, deletion, and consultant access are future production work.

## Can this support another industry?

The provider-neutral core accepts typed domain rules and does not contain Exabytes capability identifiers. Another reviewed domain pack can supply questions, score rules, recommendations, scenarios, advisors, and report definitions; that future implementation is not claimed complete.

## What did the team build versus third parties?

The team built the product contracts, domain model, deterministic engines, evidence/provenance system, UI journey, persistence boundaries, fallbacks, tests, and submission package. Next.js/React/Zod/Vitest/axe-core/Vercel AI SDK and design/deployment tools are declared in the third-party register.
