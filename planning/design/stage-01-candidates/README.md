# Stage 01 visual candidates

Generated with the approved Stage 01 design brief on 17 September 2026.

## Decision criteria

The main task compared each candidate for:

1. trust and suitability for Malaysian SME owners;
2. focus on one grouped question rather than dashboard distraction;
3. readability and control size;
4. fit with the navy, teal, warm off-white, and restrained coral direction;
5. likely accessibility and keyboard clarity;
6. ability to collapse naturally to a 360px mobile layout;
7. ability to extend into follow-up and Business Twin review screens;
8. absence of fabricated scores, recommendations, or misleading AI behavior.

## Comparison

| Candidate | Strengths | Main concern | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Strong hierarchy and explicit help panel | Dark, visually dense, dashboard navigation implies unbuilt features | Not selected |
| B | Polished, trustworthy, excellent runner-up | Sidebar reduces working space; controls and text become small; less natural mobile collapse | Runner-up |
| C | Clear cards and strong contrast | Dark/futuristic and multicolour; conflicts with the agreed calm light advisory direction | Not selected |
| D | Focused, bright, spacious, large controls, strongest responsive foundation | Stock hero image and draft labels must be removed or corrected during expansion | Recommended |

## Recommended direction

Candidate D is the main task's recommendation, not an irreversible decision.
Its layout and visual system should be preserved, while the expanded design must:

- replace the draft progress labels with the exact five-question structure;
- change the inaccurate 12-minute estimate to the approved 2-3 minute core interview target;
- use the exact eight digital-foundation capability fields;
- remove or reduce the stock-photo hero so it does not compete with the form;
- add the follow-up and Business Twin review states;
- preserve explicit local-save wording and accessible control states.
